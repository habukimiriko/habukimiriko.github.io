What is this?

A PGP (Pretty Good Privacy) signature is a digital signature that ensures that you can easily identify the authenticity of any message posted by me, as long as they come with a PGP signature. A PGP Public Key is the necessary data needed to de-encrypt the digital signature, and a seperate Private Key is used to encrypt the original data into the signature.
