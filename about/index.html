Personal

My name, Habuki, is derived from the kanji "Hane", that is, a feather, and "buki", to blow, to fly.

I spend most of my spare time reading. Thanks to this habit, I've acquired a lot of esoteric and obscure knowledge, and I'm passionate about a lot of them. Yes, I am slightly autistic. I am neurodivergent, and I am proud of it.

Apparently, I am known to like "Dark literature", Kafka, Akutagawa, etc. Also apparently, my fashion style is very "exotic", as pointed out by several of my close friends. A picture is attached so you can evaluate by your own judgement.

Visual Novels, if they count as games. I have spent a copious amount of time on those, fortunately or not.
